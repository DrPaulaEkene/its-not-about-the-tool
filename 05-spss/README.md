# SPSS

**Status: not built yet.**

The intention is to put a proper statistical test behind two of the claims the dashboards make by eye: the gap between men and women, and whether the deprivation gradient holds up as a trend rather than a coincidence.

[`syntax.sps`](syntax.sps) is the prepared syntax file. It has not been run, so there are no results here and nothing in it should be quoted.

Worth saying plainly: the confidence intervals already published by ONS do most of the work on the deprivation question. The poorest fifth and the richest fifth do not overlap, which is why that four times figure is in the dashboards at all.
